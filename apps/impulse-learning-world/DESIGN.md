# DESIGN.md — Impulse Learning World

## Visual World: "Sunlit Expedition Atlas"
- **Surface Mode:** Experience + Operate hybrid — the Journey Map and Lesson Runner feel like a warm, illustrated 2.5D travel RPG; Quests, Ranks, and Progress Dashboard remain crisp, scannable, and distraction-free.
- **Ground & Ink:** Warm Atlas Parchment (`#F5EFE4`), Crisp Storybook Ivory cards (`#FFFDF9`), Deep Expedition Ink (`#181C2E`) for primary copy, Warm Slate (`#545A72`) for secondary copy. Dark mode switches cleanly to Midnight Observatory (`#0E1220` ground, `#171C30` surface).
- **World Region Accents:**
  - `A1` Brighthaven Bay: Harbor Teal (`#0D9488`) + Coral (`#EA580C`)
  - `A2` London Skyrail: Royal Cobalt (`#2563EB`) + Tram Crimson (`#E11D48`)
  - `B1` Kyoto Lantern District: Lantern Magenta (`#DB2777`) + Warm Amber (`#D97706`)
  - `B2` Summit Observatory: Astral Indigo (`#4F46E5`) + Aurora Emerald (`#059669`)
- **Game Economy Accents:** Coins (`#D97706`), Gems (`#0891B2`), Streak (`#EA580C`), XP (`#10B981`).
- **Tactile Game Polish:** Illustrated 2.5D city banners, curved SVG expedition trail connecting interactive pulsing lesson nodes, tactile 3D-bezel primary action buttons, animated XP/progress bars, zero eyebrow clutter, and consistent Lucide iconography (no emoji icons).
