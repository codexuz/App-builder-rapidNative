# Visual Direction: Neo-brutalist / Creator

- **Best for:** Creator tools, music/event discovery, indie social apps, streetwear, experimental utilities.
- **Palette:** High-contrast bone/chalk ground (`#F6F3EA`) with pitch ink (`#111111`), crisp `2px` solid borders, acid lime / electric orange / cobalt spot accents.
- **Typography:** Heavy, unapologetic display headings (`800–900` weight), tight tracking, raw utilitarian labels and monospace metadata where earned.
- **Layout:** Distinct architectural blocks, sharp or tightly controlled radii (`8–12pt`), intentional hard offset shadows ONLY in this direction, bold typographic headers.
- **Motion:** Fast, tactile snap transitions (`100–180ms`), zero mushy fades.
- **Avoid:** Soft blurred shadows, pastel-on-pastel low contrast, mixing glassmorphism into a brutalist system.
