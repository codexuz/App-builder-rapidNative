# Visual Direction: Editorial / Swiss

- **Best for:** Reading apps, news, journalism, productivity, curated commerce, learning guides.
- **Palette:** Warm archival paper (`#F7F5F0`) or crisp gallery white (`#FFFFFF`), deep charcoal ink (`#141413`), muted stone dividers (`#E2DDD3`), one disciplined editorial accent (vermilion, cobalt, or forest ink).
- **Typography:** Strong scale contrast between display headlines (`30–36pt`, tight tracking `-0.6`) and calm, breathable body (`15–16pt`, generous line height). Tabular numerals for dates and figures.
- **Layout:** Asymmetric grids, hairline rules instead of heavy boxes, generous negative space, typography carrying hierarchy rather than nested cards.
- **Motion:** Restrained, crisp easing (`fast` / `normal`), subtle fade-and-lift entrances; avoid bouncy game physics.
- **Avoid:** Heavy drop shadows, neon glows, decorative glassmorphism, repetitive rounded cards for every section.
