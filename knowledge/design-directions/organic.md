# Visual Direction: Organic / Lifestyle

- **Best for:** Wellness, mindfulness, recipes, travel journals, outdoor clubs, boutique hospitality.
- **Palette:** Warm sand (`#FAF6F0`), sage, terracotta (`#C2410C`), espresso ink (`#1E1915`), soft linen cards (`#FFFDF9`), natural photography and warm editorial illustrations.
- **Typography:** Calm, unhurried rhythm, generous line spacing, soft warmth without sacrificing >= 4.5:1 contrast.
- **Layout:** Full-bleed imagery with clean scrims, soft generous radii (`18–26pt`), breathing room around focal cards, calm bottom sheets.
- **Motion:** Slow, serene `gentle` springs (`damping: 22, stiffness: 110`), breathing/ambient progress rings, gentle crossfades.
- **Avoid:** Harsh neon accents, crowded metric tables, aggressive notification badges.
