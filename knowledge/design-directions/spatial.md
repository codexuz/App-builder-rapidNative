# Visual Direction: Spatial / Futuristic

- **Best for:** AI assistants, voice companions, spatial audio, sci-fi entertainment, next-gen fitness wearables.
- **Palette:** Deep midnight canvas (`#070912`), layered translucent-feeling dark surfaces (`#111626`, `#192038`), crisp ice-white typography, controlled luminous cyan/violet/emerald accents used sparingly as focal light sources.
- **Typography:** Clean geometric sans, crisp wide-tracked micro-labels only for telemetry/data, clear contrast hierarchy.
- **Layout:** Immersive focal centerpiece (orb, waveform, ring, or live canvas), minimal chrome, floating bottom controls, deliberate dark-first atmosphere.
- **Motion:** Fluid continuous waveforms, smooth spring transitions, ambient pulse cues respecting reduced-motion settings.
- **Avoid:** Generic purple-blue startup gradients everywhere, illegible grey-on-black text, overusing blur on every card.
